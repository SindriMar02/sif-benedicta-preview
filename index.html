ABOUT +

A fusion of international experience and Icelandic sensibilities

The talented Icelandic designer Halldóra Sif brings forth a fusion of international experience and Icelandic sensibilities. From her intern days at Olympia-Le-Tan in the heart of Paris, to her stint with the iconic Alexander McQueen in the vibrant streets of London, her journey has been a whirlwind of creativity and exploration.

Benedicta’s Room, the debut collection, draws inspiration from the vibrant colors and bold forms of 60’s pop art, as well as the elegance of Art Deco and Art Nouveau. Benedicta’s Sketchbook, the second collection, is inspired by the intricate designs of Art Deco jewelry and wallpaper, vintage book covers, a fantasy of 20’s glamour and romantic 70’s chic.
